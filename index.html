Station dashboards

A screen on the apparatus bay wall that takes dispatch data from Active911 and puts it where crews can see it. No special hardware contracts and nothing to install on your computers.

Alerts that stand out

Call type, address, units, map and street view fill the screen. Colors follow call priority, and a turnout timer starts when the alert arrives.

Useful between calls

The screen rotates through your department slides, a live weather radar, and the last 24 hours of incidents, along with the current shift.

Low maintenance

Displays update themselves and can be refreshed or cleared remotely, so no one has to visit the station to fix a screen.
