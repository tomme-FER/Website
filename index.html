The dashboard

Everything the crew needs, on one screen

A display at the station that takes dispatch data from Active911 and puts it in front of the crew. The screens below show sample data.

Instant incident alerts

When a call comes in from Active911, the display immediately shows the incident type, address, and units assigned, along with a Google map and Street View when available. Colors follow call priority, and a turnout timer starts when the alert arrives.

Recent incidents, current shift and time

Between calls the display keeps a list of past incidents so crews can see what the station has run, with the shift on duty and the current time always in view.

Weather and weather alerts

Current weather is always on the header, and active weather alerts are displayed on screen so crews see warnings without leaving the station display.

All screens show sample data, not a real department or incident.
