For IT departments

What your station and IT team need

Simple for most stations. Here are the details if your IT department wants them.

What we install

A small display computer at the station, connected to a TV or monitor by HDMI. It shows the dashboard full screen. It only makes outbound connections, the same way a web browser does.

What the station needs to provide

  • A TV or monitor with an HDMI input.
  • A power outlet near the TV that stays on (not a switched circuit).
  • A network connection. A wired Ethernet connection is the most reliable. Wi-Fi also works.

For your IT department

  • Outbound only. No inbound connections, port forwarding or public IP address are needed.
  • Web traffic (HTTPS, port 443) to our dashboard service, to weather and radar data from the National Weather Service, and to map and presentation services. We will give your IT staff the exact list of host names for your site before installation.
  • Internet time (NTP) is not required. The display sets its clock from our service when internet time is blocked.
  • Wi-Fi: if Wi-Fi is used, a stable network is best. A wired Ethernet connection avoids signal and roaming problems.
  • Lost or retired devices can be shut off from our side, so a screen cannot be reused by someone else.

Questions

If your IT team has security or network questions, email contact@fireemsreporting.com and we will answer them before anything is installed.
